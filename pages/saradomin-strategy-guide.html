Overview

Saradomin's projection is a multi-way group boss fought on the Mirror Hub – an instanced copy of the Hub island that serves as the anchor for his power. The fight is shared: everyone sent to the island fights the same boss in the same instance, and every attack he makes strikes every player present, not just his target.

The encounter is forgiving to attempt (death is safe and any teleport is an exit) but punishes autopilot play: his signature attack turns your own prayer points into damage, and from 70% health he begins praying, reflecting damage, freezing, and repositioning.

Getting there

First visit: speak to the Isotope Guide at the Hub and ask how to fight back against Saradomin. He directs you to Wizard Traiborn at the tower, who offers a boat to "the source of Saradomin's power". Take the boat. The voyage does not go as advertised; you will wash ashore on the Mirror Hub, and the projection will gather from a column of light to meet you.

Returning (skip the story): once you have survived a visit, you can confront Traiborn – "You sent me to him. You KNEW." After he confirms it, his dialogue permanently drops the act and offers Send me to him. Now. You can also demand he grant his sending-power to the Isotope Guide; after that, the Guide's fight-back option becomes a direct Send me to the mirror.

The instance persists while anyone is inside and for 3 minutes after it empties, then resets. The boss respawns 60 seconds after each kill while players remain.

Messages

You drag yourself ashore... but this island looks like the Hub itself. His prayer turns aside half of your spells! Holy chains root you in place! Your own prayers are turned against you! Saradomin closes the distance on YOU!
  • The state of the island and the projection – arrivals, his death, and his return.
  • One of his mechanics has taken hold of you – your damage is being halved, or you have been rooted.
  • Respond immediately – a Judgement has landed on your prayer pool, or the closing blink has chosen you and his next attack is coming at point-blank.

Strategy

Prayer management is the fight

Divine Judgement's damage is your current prayer points divided by four (three, enraged) on top of a flat base, and it pierces protection prayers – so a full prayer bar is a weapon pointed at you. Two workable approaches:

Low-prayer method: fight with a modest pool (enough to flick protects) and let his drains keep it low. Judgement then hits close to its base only.

Full-protect method: keep Protect from Magic up for the barrages and eat the Judgements. Budget supplies for hits over 200 mid-enrage, and expect the one-third drain to eat your restores.

Learn the rotations

He no longer rolls dice – each phase repeats a fixed cycle (Sermon: Barrage, Barrage, Judgement; Tempest adds Ice; Host trims back to three). Count his casts and you always know the next attack, which makes prayer flicking genuinely rewarding rather than reactive.

The Chains decision

Divine Chains is where Protect from Magic earns double duty: it halves his barrages AND gives you a 50% resist against the root. Resist, and you skip the Defence sap, eat only 2 volleys, and he comes to you; get chained, and it is sap plus 3 volleys – switch to Protect from Missiles the moment you see the chains, because ranged exists nowhere else in the fight.

The Host

His entire defensive kit arrives at 50% together: knights, rotating prayers (re-rolled every 6 attacks – swap style on his callout or accept half damage), and Vengeance, cast silently with only the spell graphic as a tell.

Phase 3: the host

The knights are pure melee – Protect from Melee blanks them entirely, but that competes with the protect you want against his barrages. In a group, one player draws and holds the knights under Protect from Melee at the arena edge while the rest stay on the boss; solo, killing the wave is now doubly worthwhile: they die permanently until the ~25 second renewal, buying a clean window, and every living knight multiplies his Vengeance reflection (x1.5 with one, x3 with two – a 300 you land into a two-knight Vengeance comes back as 675). Do not let the knights stack on you during a rooted Repositioning – that is where phase 3 kills people.

Do not bring Vengeance

If your own Vengeance recoils against him, he reflects that recoil back at you fourfold. A Vengeance that returns 200 of his barrage becomes an 800 on your own head – comfortably lethal. Lunar spellbook users: leave the habit at the dock.

The closing blink

The end of kite mode targets a random player, not whoever he was attacking. If the red warning names you, you have his next attack incoming at point-blank – keep a protect up and be ready to eat.

Enrage

From 35% he attacks faster and the barrage becomes two bolts – the second bolt is unaffected by the first being blocked, so Protect from Magic matters more, not less. Don't sit at 50% food "for later"; the last third of the fight is where deaths happen.

Group encounters

The boss does not gain life points with group size, but every attack hits every player – a bigger team kills faster yet consumes more total supplies. Since the closing blink and Judgement ignore aggro, there is no true tank role; everyone prays and eats for themselves.

Knights make group play meaningfully easier: a designated off-tank praying melee can hold the entire wave at no supply cost while everyone else ignores phase 3. Solo players must treat the knight windows with respect.

Bones and the resource roll are per-participant and uncontested. The rare rolls are different: one roll per kill, one recipient, with each participant's odds proportional to the damage they dealt – carrying the fight raises your chances, leeching lowers them. A larger team therefore kills faster without producing more shard hoards or godswords per kill.

Late joiners enter the same instance mid-fight through Traiborn or the Guide. Leaving (teleport, death, or logout) removes you from the fight; the island resets 3 minutes after the last player leaves.

The Enchanted Saradomin godsword requires 75 Attack. It carries roughly 12% higher bonuses than the godsword line, and its special attack (50% adrenaline) deals 1.2x damage, heals for 60% of the hit, and restores prayer from a third of it – the projection drops the one blade built to survive his own Judgement.

Trivia

• Nothing Wizard Traiborn says before the betrayal is technically false – including "you will stand before the god himself."

• The Isotope Guide admits he has never met Traiborn when he first mentions him. Listen closely.

• The projection displays no combat level. The Hub's scholars maintain that gods do not have one; the Hub's warriors maintain that he is hiding it.

• The ground within ten paces of his anchor is scoured bare of trees and brush – the light tolerates no cover.
